# Sources and verification policy

## Priority reference sources
1. Oxford Learner's Dictionaries — Oxford 3000/5000 and UK pronunciation/definitions: https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/wordlists/oxford3000-5000/
2. Cambridge Dictionary — British pronunciation, definitions and usage: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/
3. Collins English Dictionary: https://www.collinsdictionary.com/
4. Longman Dictionary of Contemporary English: https://www.ldoceonline.com/

## Record-level evidence
For every published entry, record at least one source that directly supports the UK pronunciation and core meaning. Use additional sources for important multiple senses, disputed level assignment or pronunciation variants.

Do not invent source URLs, IPA, CEFR labels or dictionary confirmations. If a source cannot be checked, set verification_status to needs_review and explain the gap in notes.

## UK pronunciation
Target contemporary Standard Southern British English (SSBE). Preserve lexical stress and vowel length. Distinguish UK forms from US forms. Where reputable sources show more than one UK pronunciation, record the selected form and note the variant.

## CEFR levels
A0 is an editorial foundation band, not an official CEFR level. For A1–B2, prefer Oxford 3000 level labels and corroborate uncertain cases with other CEFR-aligned references. Do not infer a level solely from perceived difficulty.

## Copyright and data handling
Use sources to verify facts, not to reproduce proprietary dictionary entries or example sentences. Write original example sentences and Ukrainian translations.
